# DET mechanics audit — 3 October 2026 (Cambodia)

Official sources retrieved during this audit, not inferred from third-party prep sites. Entry point: https://testcenter.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/sections/39104552844557-Test-Structure-and-Questions

| Family | Official mechanics | Fixed beginner form |
|---|---|---|
| Read and Select | 15–18 words, 5 seconds each, selection advances | 16 distinct words |
| Fill in the Blanks | 6–9 sentences, 20 seconds each | 7, missing letters |
| Read and Complete | 3–6 passages, 3 minutes each | 4 multi-gap passages |
| Listen and Type | 6–9, 1 minute, initial audio plus two replays | 7, three plays total |
| Interactive Reading | Two six-question sets, 7 or 8 minutes shared per set | 7 minutes; sentence gaps, passage gap, two evidence highlights, idea, title |
| Interactive Listening | Two sets; 3–4 scenario blanks then replies; total 8–10 questions, shared 6:30 | Two sets of three blanks and six replies |
| Listening summary | Separate 1:15 after each conversation | Two paragraphs |
| Write About the Photo | Three, 1 minute each | Three distinct illustrations; photographic fidelity limited |
| Interactive Writing | 30s prep, 5-minute first response, 3-minute follow-up; initial response visible | Fixed related follow-up |
| Speak About the Photo | 20s prep, 90s response | Illustration with recording |
| Read, Then Speak | 20s prep, 90s response | Written prompt with recording |
| Interactive Speaking | 6–8 audio-only questions, one play, 35s response each | Six fixed prompts; no animated character/adaptive selection |
| Writing Sample | 30s prep, 5-minute response | Written response |
| Speaking Sample | 30s prep, 3-minute response | Audio only; real sample includes video |

The certified test needs approximately one hour and adapts both difficulty and item count. This fixed mock is practice, has shorter beginner passages and simplified multi-gap interaction. It cannot reproduce official scoring, partial credit or answer-sensitive adaptive prompts. Exact-match feedback may mark valid listening paraphrases as mismatches; use the target as a reference, not an official mark. Local normalized diagnostics ignore case and basic punctuation. Summary and other open responses need human self-review with rubrics. Speech synthesis varies by device and may not work offline.

## Release boundary
Suitable as an independent beginner practice coach, not a certified DET replica or a guaranteed score preparation course. Serve over HTTPS or localhost for microphone support. No backend, user accounts, cloud sync, AI assessment, resumable timed mock, or durable recording storage. Typed responses, drafts, interrupted attempt and progress stay in local browser storage. Close the coach during the certified test. Confirm the current official rules, secondary phone camera setup, and institution score requirement separately.
